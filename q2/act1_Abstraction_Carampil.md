Activity 1: The Digital Scale (Electronic Balance) Analysis (5 mins)
Goal: Distinguish between the Interface (what we use) and the Implementation (what is hidden).
    The Abstraction What are the only 2 things a scientist actually needs to touch to get a result? Think before reading further.

Power Button: Turns the digital scale on or off.
Tare/Zero Button: Resets the displayed measurement to zero, allowing the scientist to exclude the weight of the container.
Display Screen: Shows the final measured mass in grams.

2.The Complexity What are 3 "hidden" things happening inside the box that we don't need to understand to weigh our salt (or sample)? Think before reading further.

Load Cell: A metal sensor that detects weight by slightly deforming when an object is placed on the scale.
Signal Processing: Converts the small electrical signals produced by the sensor into readable digital measurements.
Calibration Math: Uses internal calculations to ensure that the displayed measurements are accurate and reliable.
